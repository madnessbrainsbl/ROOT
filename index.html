ONE LOCAL WORKFLOW

From weakness to verified remediation

RØOT is not a live vulnerable target and does not send your work to a third-party service. Its simulations teach the reasoning behind common application-security failures in a controlled browser environment.

01

Learn

Read bilingual OWASP Web, API, and LLM security material with CWE mappings and practical context.

02

Exploit

Work through safe client-side simulations, capture flags, and use progressive hints when you are stuck.

03

Fix

Compare vulnerable and remediated code, then verify the lesson with a structured quiz.

04

Investigate

Search a reproducible offline snapshot derived from CISA KEV and CVE List V5.
